rework the model into more of a simulation and less of a hard coded equation.

The goal should be to maximize the sum of weekly starter values over the next three years. we must assume that our
projections bake risks like injury and demotion in, but unfortunately they are too coarse to be useful: injuries are
spiky while our projections are season-long averages. Injuries don't reduce the average of all games for a season by 4; 
they remove some number of games completely, potentially followed by a ramp up period. 
that matters for starting lineups and backups. 
but we can't add simulated injuries on top of projections because they would be double counted, 
affecting positional differences. injuries would also cause a commiserate increase in backup scores during the relevant weeks.

Bye weeks on the other hand we can apply (exactly for year one, randomly for future years), because they affect all players
equally.
